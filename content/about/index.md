## About This Blog

This is where I document my homelab journey — from initial hardware decisions to the inevitable
3am debugging sessions when something breaks.

Topics covered here include:

- **Home networking** — VLANs, firewall rules, DNS, and everything in between
- **Self-hosting** — Running your own services instead of relying on third parties
- **Hardware** — Servers, switches, access points, and NAS builds
- **Automation** — Ansible, Terraform, and scripting the mundane away
- **Monitoring** — Knowing what your network is doing at all times
